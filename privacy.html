Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Doana Digital collects, uses and protects information submitted through our website.

Last updated: August 2026

01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Doana Digital website and our online forms.

Doana Digital is a creative studio providing branding, graphic design, social media design, marketing materials and other digital and creative services.

02

Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or submit feedback. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business or organization name
  • The service you are interested in
  • Project descriptions, requirements and timelines
  • Client reviews, ratings and testimonials
  • Other information you choose to include in a message or form
03

How we use your information

Information submitted to Doana Digital may be used to:

  • Respond to project inquiries
  • Communicate with potential and existing clients
  • Understand project requirements
  • Prepare quotes or discuss potential services
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Manage client feedback and testimonials
  • Maintain the security and operation of our website

We do not sell your personal information.

04

Contact inquiries

When you submit a project inquiry through our website, the information you provide is used to review your request and communicate with you about the project.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive information through our contact forms.

05

Client feedback

Visitors and clients may have the option to submit feedback or testimonials through the Doana Digital website.

Submitted reviews may be reviewed or moderated before publication. A review will not necessarily appear publicly immediately after submission.

If approved for publication, information submitted as part of a testimonial, such as your name, business name, rating and review text, may be displayed publicly on our website.

06

Service providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate parts of our website, process form submissions, store information, provide website hosting or maintain website functionality.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services.

07

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to inquiries, maintaining client relationships, keeping appropriate business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

09

Your privacy choices

You may contact Doana Digital if you have questions about personal information you have submitted through our website.

You may also contact us to request that we correct or delete information where appropriate, subject to applicable legal and business requirements.

10

External links

Our website may contain links to external platforms such as Instagram and LinkedIn.

Once you leave the Doana Digital website, the privacy practices of those external services apply. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12

Questions about privacy?

If you have questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or information you submitted to Doana Digital, please contact us.
